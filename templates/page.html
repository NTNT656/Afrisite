2023

A Solo Voice: Mr. Extrodin Thinks

It started with Khutso Mataboge — known as KEM, or Mr. Extrodin — wanting a podcast that actually dissected movies. He began with solo reviews and trailer talks on films like The Flash, Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse, Air, and Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: Mutant Mayhem. The name African Frame didn't exist yet; all of it lived on the Mr. Extrodin Thinks YouTube channel.

2023

Bringing In Roberto

Khutso had known Vuyisile Roberto Sibeko since 2014, and had long admired Roberto's WhatsApp statuses about his own film projects like God's Mandate, plus his deep movie knowledge and scriptwriting. It was clear Roberto belonged on the podcast.

2023

The Name Is Born

Together they launched a weekly show dissecting the work of Christopher Nolan, Quentin Tarantino, Brian De Palma, and more. Roberto pushed for structure — writing out each film breakdown beforehand instead of reviewing in a non-linear, freeform way. The new flow worked, and out of it came the name African Frame. It began as a playlist before Khutso migrated it to its own channel, first called Mr. Extrodin, then renamed African Frame.

May 2024

Cinema SodZA Is Born

Khutso's habit of checking the Numetro app for what was screening sparked an idea: a show built around movies currently in theatres. What began as The Numetro Show became Cinema SodZA — checking that month's theatrical releases, breaking down trailers, and making box office predictions.

2024

Box Office Clash Splits Off

Predictions needed a payoff, so the team started revisiting them a month later against real box office results. That comparison outgrew its segment and became its own show: Box Office Clash. Both started as playlists on the African Frame channel until Roberto pushed for Cinema SodZA to become its own YouTube channel — now home to Cinema SodZA, Box Office Clash, 7UP, and Crisis Event.

2024

Weekly Pics

Roberto wanted a format where each host watched and reviewed a film independently, without the other seeing it first — a way to keep reviews shorter and unfiltered. Weekly Pics was born.

2024

7UP

Roberto's focus on movie franchises led to 7 and Up — 7UP for short — a show built around film franchises with seven or more entries, covering the Marvel Cinematic Universe, Mission: Impossible, the DC Extended Universe, and beyond.

2024

Series Nton Nton

Not wanting series to go uncovered, Khutso launched Series Nton Nton — a channel dedicated entirely to series reviews and breakdowns.

2024

Crisis Event

Over the years, guests with a genuine passion for film became regulars — Zanozuko Mlambo, Khetho Borole (who became a permanent co-host), Tebello Khala, Alize, Kagiso (Dude The Movie Guy), and Cole from Movie Doctor. When several of them landed in the Cinema SodZA WhatsApp group, Roberto had an idea: gather everyone, Justice League-style, for a laid-back conversation about the latest movies. Named after the DC Comics event of the same name, Crisis Event was born.

Where It's Headed

What started as trailer talk on a solo YouTube channel has grown into African Frame, Cinema SodZA, African Frame Plus, and Series Nton Nton — four channels, seven shows, and a rotating cast of guests who just can't stop talking about film. The passion for movies is what got Khutso and Roberto here, and it's what keeps them building — with one goal in mind: to become the biggest movie platform in Africa.
