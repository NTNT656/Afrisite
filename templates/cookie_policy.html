Cookie Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and improve your experience.

How We Use Cookies

African Frame uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies: These are necessary for the website to function properly, e.g., to keep you logged in to the admin panel.
  • Analytics cookies: We use these to understand how visitors interact with our site (e.g., page views, clicks) so we can improve content. We use anonymous analytics tools.

Your Choices

You can control and manage cookies in your browser settings. You can also withdraw your consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies. However, disabling essential cookies may affect some functionality.

Third-Party Cookies

We may embed videos from YouTube and other platforms, which may set their own cookies. Please refer to those platforms' privacy policies for details.

Contact

If you have any questions about our cookie usage, please contact us.
